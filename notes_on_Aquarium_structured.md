**Coconut preparation (mandatory before first use):**
1. Rinse under strong cold running water to remove transport dust.
2. Place all shells in a pot, cover with water, bring to a boil, simmer for **15 minutes**.
3. Water will turn dark brown — this is normal (tannins releasing). This prevents them from tinting your aquarium water.
4. Pour off the brown water, cover with cold water and wait until fully cool.
5. Place in sand and anchor lightly by pressing sand around the edges.

## 5. Cycling & Tank Start-Up

### 5.1 Process Overview

1. Tank decorated and filled (mid-May 2026).
2. **Ghost feeding** — dropped tropical flakes into empty tank daily for ~2 weeks to generate ammonia.
3. **Seachem Stability** dosed daily (15–25 ml) to seed beneficial bacteria.
4. **Nitrite spike** occurred — waited it out without water changes (empty tank, no fish to protect).
5. Full aeration run at maximum the entire time.
6. NO2 dropped to 0 by ~2 June 2026 — cycle complete.
7. **First fish added: Tuesday 2 June 2026.**

### 5.2 Why Not to Do a Water Change During Cycling (Empty Tank)

- Nitrite IS the food for the slow-growing second bacteria population (Nitrobacter).
- Removing 30% of water removes 30% of their food source, slowing the cycle.
- Only do emergency water changes during cycling if fish are present and NO2 is dangerously high.

### 5.3 Why NO2 Spikes Happen

1. Ghost feeding residue — flakes rot → ammonia → nitrite → nitrate
2. No weekly water changes to export nitrate
3. Overfeeding (future risk — leave no uneaten food on sand)
4. Decaying plant matter (remove yellowing leaves promptly)
5. Irish tap water contains trace nitrates from agricultural run-off

## 6. Chemicals & Dosing

### 6.1 Seachem Stability (Bacterial Supplement)

| Phase | Dose | Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Cycling phase (empty tank) | 25–30 ml/day | Every morning until NO2 = 0 |
| After adding Step 1 fish (2 June) | 30 ml/day | Each morning Thu 4 June → Sun 7 June, then STOP |
| After each subsequent step | 25 ml once | Day of new fish addition only |
| After filter maintenance | 25 ml once | Immediately after reassembly |
| After medication treatment | 25 ml/day | 3 days after treatment finishes |

> **Note:** Cannot be overdosed — safe for all inhabitants including shrimp and plants. Pour directly over/in front of the filter outlet so the current carries it into the media.

### 6.2 Seachem Prime (Water Conditioner / Dechlorinator)

- **Use only during water changes** — add to fresh tap water before adding to tank.
- Dose: 2–3 drops per bucket of fresh water, or ~1 ml per full water change.
- Emergency use: If NO2 rises while fish are in the tank, add 5 ml directly to the tank — Prime neutralises NO2 toxicity for 24–48 hours.

### 6.3 Easy-Life ProFito (Plant Fertiliser)

- **Do not use** during Steps 1–3 (plants feed from substrate/fish waste during this phase).
- Start: Mid-July 2026, the day after a water change.
- Dose: Half the manufacturer-recommended dose once per week.
- Substrate capsules: Push one capsule deep near Echinodorus/Cryptocoryne roots every 6 months.

## 8. Maintenance Calendar

### 8.1 Daily (1 minute)

- **Morning:** Visual check — water flowing from filter, bubbles active, temperature at 25–26°C on heater display.
- **Evening:** Feed fish (see feeding schedule above).

### 8.2 Weekly — Every Thursday (~20 minutes)

- **Water change:** Remove 3 buckets of water (~30 litres, ~30–40% of tank).
- **Dechlorinate:** Add 2–3 drops Seachem Prime to each bucket of fresh tap water; mix; pour into tank.
- **Front glass:** Wipe inside of front glass with a clean magnetic cleaner or a brand-new kitchen sponge (no soap, no detergent).
- **Plants:** Trim any yellow, transparent, or decaying leaves at the base.

### 8.3 Monthly — Last Saturday of Month (~10 minutes)

- **Gravel vacuum:** Use a siphon tube to lightly skim the surface of the white sand in feeding areas. Do not dig deep — only remove surface detritus.

### 8.4 Every 3 Months — Filter Service (~20 minutes)

**Next scheduled service: Saturday 29 August 2026**

> **CRITICAL: Never service the filter on the same day as a water change. Offset by at least 2–3 days.**

**Procedure:**
1. Unplug filter from mains. Close the AquaStop valve (red lever).
2. Carry the canister to the bathroom.
3. **Pre-filter foams:** Rinse black coarse foams under lukewarm tap water until clean.
4. **Ceramic media (NEVER use tap water):** Decant a bowl of old aquarium water first. Gently swirl the ceramic baskets inside the bowl to dislodge loose sludge. Do not scrub.
5. Reassemble filter, prime with aquarium water, reconnect.
6. Dose one capful (25 ml) of Seachem Stability directly into the tank to replenish disturbed colonies.

### 8.5 Every 6 Months / Annually

- **Filter hoses:** If water flow noticeably decreases, clean the ribbed hoses with a flexible brush.
- **Substrate fertiliser capsules:** Push one capsule deep into the sand near Echinodorus (Amazon Sword) and Cryptocoryne roots.

## 9. Water Change Protocol (Step-by-Step)

This protocol applies to every weekly Thursday water change:

1. Switch off the filter and heater.
2. Use a bucket and siphon hose to remove **3 buckets** (~30 L) of tank water.
3. While draining, gently wipe the inner front glass with a clean, soap-free sponge.
4. Fill 3 buckets with fresh tap water.
5. Add **2–3 drops of Seachem Prime** to each bucket. Stir.
6. Pour fresh water slowly into the tank (aim at a rock or wall to avoid disturbing sand).
7. Switch heater and filter back on.
8. After filling, pour 25 ml Seachem Stability over the filter outlet (optional but recommended).

## 10. Acclimatisation Protocol (Adding New Fish)

Use this procedure every time fish are brought home from Seahorse Aquariums:

1. **Do not turn on lights.** Leave the Fluval Aquasky completely OFF for the night.
2. Float the sealed store bags on the water surface for **20 minutes** (temperature equalisation to 25.5°C).
3. Open the bags. Add half a glass of tank water to each bag. Wait 10 minutes.
4. Net the fish out of the bags and release into the tank.
5. **Pour the store water down the sink — never add it to your tank.**
6. Pour 25 ml Seachem Stability directly over the filter outlet.
7. **Do not feed** that evening. Give the first feeding the next morning.
8. Run the air pump at maximum for the first 24 hours.
9. Turn the Aquasky back on the next morning (resume normal schedule).

## 12. Observations & Notes

- **Gourami surface territory:** Pearl Gourami are labyrinth fish — they need to reach the surface to breathe air. When added, redirect the filter outflow downward or to the side glass to reduce surface turbulence. Leave one calm corner (near the Amazon Sword) free of bubbles for nest-building.
- **Pearl Gourami quantity:** Keep exactly 2 (a mated pair). Two males will fight aggressively and the weaker one will die. Maximum is a harem of 1 male + 2 females, but a pair is safest.
- **White biofilm on new driftwood:** Normal. Amano Shrimp will consume it within days.
- **Coconut shells turn water brown:** Normal in the first few days after boiling. The tannins are released during boiling, not in the tank.
- **Test strips vs. liquid tests:** Test strips read at 30 seconds are reliable. Strips that dry out or are left standing show artificially darker colours. The shop at Seahorse Aquariums may use liquid reagents; a discrepancy between their result and a fresh strip test is likely due to expired store reagents or old sample water.
- **Irish tap water note:** County Meath and Dublin tap water naturally contains trace nitrates from agricultural fertiliser run-off. Always test tap water, not just tank water, if nitrates seem unexpectedly high after a water change.
- **Panda Garra timing:** Must be added last (Step 4, July) because they need natural biofilm and algae on rocks/wood to feed on, and to avoid stressing the new Apistogramma pair in Step 2.
- **NO2 vs NO3:** Nitrite (NO2) is immediately lethal — blocks oxygen transport in blood. Nitrate (NO3) is safe up to ~40 ppm and is consumed by plants. Always prioritise getting NO2 to 0 before adding any fish.
- **Shopping:** Seahorse Aquariums, Dublin — offers free water tests (bring a jar of tank water). Always get a free test before each new purchase.
