# Project Nemo — Supplies & Consumables

This file feeds directly into the app's **inventory / dosing system**.
When you first set up the app, enter the starting volume for each liquid and the app will track usage automatically.

---

## Liquid Chemicals — Tracked by App (Daily/Per-Use Dosing)

### 1. Seachem Prime
- **What:** Water conditioner / dechlorinator. Neutralises chlorine, chloramine, ammonia, and heavy metals in Irish tap water.
- **When:** Every single water change. Must treat new bucket water BEFORE adding to tank.
- **Dose:** 5 ml per 200L of new water added. For a 25L bucket: ~0.625 ml (round to 1 ml cap-full for safety).
- **Also use:** Add a double dose (2 ml per 200L) if NH3 or NO2 spikes — Prime temporarily detoxifies ammonia.
- **Bottle size:** Typically 250 ml or 500 ml.
- **App tracking:** Deduct per water change. Alert when < 50 ml remaining.
- **Buy:** Any aquarium shop, Amazon, Seahorse Aquariums Dublin.

### 2. Seachem Stability
- **What:** Liquid nitrifying bacteria. Seeds the filter with beneficial bacteria.
- **When to use:**
  - Step 0 (right now): Daily for 7 days
  - After each new fish addition (Stages 1, 2, 3): Daily for 7 days
  - After any major filter clean: Add a dose
- **Dose:** 5 ml per 40L on Day 1, then 5 ml per 80L on Days 2–7.
  - For 252L: ~32 ml Day 1, ~16 ml Days 2–7.
- **Bottle size:** Typically 250 ml or 500 ml.
- **App tracking:** Deduct per dose. Alert before Seahorse trip (May 30) and before each stocking stage.
- **Note:** Shake well before use. Can overdose safely — excess just dies off harmlessly.
- **Buy:** Seahorse Aquariums Dublin, Amazon, Zooplus.

### 3. Easy-Life ProFito (Plant Fertiliser)
- **What:** Complete liquid plant fertiliser — macro + micro nutrients including iron.
- **When to use:**
  - Step 0 (now → May 30): Half-dose weekly, to help plants adapt
  - After May 30 (fish in tank): Full dose weekly
- **Dose (full):** 5 ml per 100L = ~12.5 ml for 252L weekly.
- **Dose (half, Step 0):** ~6 ml for 252L weekly.
- **Bottle size:** Typically 250 ml or 500 ml.
- **App tracking:** Deduct weekly. Reminder every 7 days.
- **Note:** Contains iron — monitor iron test results after dosing. Target: 0.05–0.1 mg/L.
- **Buy:** Aquarium shops, Amazon, Zooplus.

### 4. Easy-Life EasyCarbo (Liquid Carbon)
- **What:** Liquid carbon supplement for planted tanks. Promotes plant growth, inhibits algae.
- **When to START:** NOT before May 30th. Begin after fish are added and plants are actively growing.
- **Dose:** 1 ml per 50L daily = ~5 ml per day for 252L.
- **Bottle size:** Typically 250 ml or 500 ml.
- **App tracking:** Deduct daily. This is the highest-consumption liquid — alert when < 50 ml.
- **CAUTION:** Toxic to shrimp and snails at overdose. Never exceed 1 ml per 50L. Your Amano shrimp and Nerite snails are sensitive — stick to exact dose.
- **Buy:** Aquarium shops, Amazon, Zooplus.

## Food — Tracked by App (Weekly Stock Check)

### 5. Generic Tropical Flakes (Ghost Feeding)
- **When:** Step 0 only — one small pinch every 2 days to feed cycling bacteria.
- **Stop:** After Stage 1 fish arrive (switch to proper foods below).
- **App:** Recurring "Ghost Feed" task every 2 days on Screen 1.

### 6. JBL Pronovo Tab M (Corydoras Sinking Tablets)
- **Who eats it:** Corydoras sterbai (8×), Kuhli Loach (6×), Otocinclus.
- **When:** Every other day, drop 2–3 tabs to the bottom at lights-out.
- **Buy at:** Seahorse Aquariums Dublin (Stage 1 trip) or Amazon.

### 7. JBL Pronovo Pleco Wafer XL (Pleco + Oto Food)
- **Who eats it:** Otocinclus (6×), Clown Pleco (1×).
- **When:** 1 wafer every 2–3 days, placed near driftwood at lights-out.
- **Buy at:** Seahorse Aquariums Dublin (Stage 1 trip) or Amazon.

### 8. Flake/Micro Pellet Food for Tetras and Angelfish
- **Who eats it:** 25× Rummy Nose Tetra, 4× Angelfish, Apistogramma.
- **When:** Once or twice daily. Small amount (consumed in 2 minutes).
- **Note:** Angelfish also accept the sinking tabs but mainly eat mid-water.
- **Buy:** Seahorse or Amazon (JBL Pronovo Flakedream or similar).

## Filter Consumables — Tracked by App (Maintenance Parts)

### 9. Fluval 307 Filter Media (from Fluval Value Pack)
- **Carbon + Phosphate Remover** (Basket 3) — replace every 4–6 weeks
- **Quick-Clear Polishing Pad** (Basket 4) — rinse weekly, replace monthly
- **Ceramic Biomedia** (Baskets 1+2) — **never fully replace**, only rinse 25–50% at a time in tank water (not tap water — kills bacteria)
- **App:** Track each media type as a "part" in the maintenance system. Checkboxes in the filter maintenance modal.

### 10. Fluval 307 Pre-filter Sponge or 16mm Intake Mesh Guard
- **Replace/clean:** Every 7–14 days (watch filter watt increase as warning sign).
- **App:** Maintenance task "Clean intake pre-filter" every 10 days.

### 11. pH Probe Replacement
- **When:** Every ~12 months (sooner if readings won't calibrate within 0.1 of buffer value).
- **Replacement:** DFRobot BNC pH probe, ~€20.
