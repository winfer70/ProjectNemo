## Etap A — Saturday 13 June 2026

**Shopping list:**
- 12× Raccoon Tetra (Hemigrammus pulcher) — "Bystrzyk Raccoon"
- 4× Panda Garra (Garra flavatra)
- 2× Pearl Gourami (sex based on current fish — see Gourami Matchmaking below)

**Gourami Matchmaking:**
- Take a video of current Gourami before leaving home
- Show video to store expert — they sex it
- If current fish is **male**: buy 2 females
- If current fish is **female**: buy 1 male + 1 female
- Males: longer pointed dorsal fin, bright fiery orange-red throat
- Females: shorter rounded dorsal, silver/white belly

**Optional purchase:**
- Aquaforest Liquid Artemia (Freshwater) or frozen brine shrimp — for Penta Barb colour

**Acclimation protocol:**
1. Turn Fluval Aquasky OFF before adding fish
2. Float sealed bags 15 min (temperature equalisation)
3. Drip acclimation 45 min until bag volume doubles
4. Net fish into tank — pour store water to sink (never add to tank)
5. Dose 30 ml Seachem Stability at filter outlet (uderzeniowa dawka)
6. Zero feeding Etap A evening
7. First feeding next morning (Sunday)
8. Aquasky back on next morning
9. Water change (15%, ~38L) on Sunday 21 June

## Water Change Protocol

**Change water when ANY of these conditions are met:**
1. NO2 (Nitrite) > 0 ppm
2. NO3 (Nitrate) > 25 ppm
3. NH3 (Ammonia) > 0 ppm
4. **4 weeks have passed** since last change (regardless of test results)

**Volume:** ~15% (~38L) per change — small but consistent.  
**Next scheduled:** Sunday 21 June 2026 (post Etap A settling).  
**Long-term routine:** every 2–3 weeks once fully stocked after Etap C.

**How:**
1. Bucket of tap water → add 2 ml Seachem Prime → match temp ±1°C
2. Siphon from bottom removing detritus
3. Pour treated water in slowly
